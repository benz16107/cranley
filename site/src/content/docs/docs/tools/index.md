Cranley has 78 tools. Each is a manifest plus a handler, and each page below is generated from the manifest and from a real run of the tool on a made-up test scan. A tool added to `server/threshold/tools/` appears here, in the web app, over MCP and in the CLI without further work.

| Tool | What it answers | Needs | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Aging in place](/docs/tools/aging-in-place/) | Goes through the CDC home fall-prevention checklist for the parts the scan can answer, floor by floor: clear paths, steps, stairs, the night path from bed to bathroom, and grab bars. | Wall outline, Room names | slow |
| [Amenity census](/docs/tools/amenity-census/) | Finds and counts a community's amenities in its scans (gym equipment, laundry machines, pool, lounge, business centre, mail room, playground) and sets them beside what the listing claims. | Property record, 360° photos | slow |
| [Area profile](/docs/tools/area-profile/) | Who lives around a building: population, density, household income, age mix, households, the share renting, rent and home value for its census tract, next to the county. | public.census_acs | fast |
| [Bathroom clearance](/docs/tools/bathroom-clearance/) | Checks each bathroom for a wheelchair: door, turning space, floor space at the toilet and clear of the door, wall for grab bars, and whether the shower has a step. | Wall outline, Room names | slow |
| [Camera coverage](/docs/tools/camera-coverage/) | Shows what indoor security cameras would see, which doors, windows and entry paths they cover, and the blind spots; proposes camera spots if you have none. | Wall outline, Doors and windows | fast |
| [City census](/docs/tools/city-census/) | Rolls up what has been measured across the city: share of homes a wheelchair can live in by zip code, listed-versus-measured area by property type, smoke alarm coverage by building. | Facts database | fast |
| [Commute fit](/docs/tools/commute-fit/) | Estimated drive time from where your people live to each candidate office: the median, who gets a long commute, and transit nearby. | inputs only | fast |
| [Connectivity](/docs/tools/connectivity/) | How well a place is connected: home broadband on offer (fiber, cable, how many providers), bus and rail stops within a walk, the nearest bikeway and the traffic on the nearest counted road. Offered speeds, not measured ones. | public.broadband, public.transit | fast |
| [Curb to door](/docs/tools/curb-to-door/) | Looks at the way in from outside: parking near the entrance, accessible stalls if the aerial photo shows them, how far it is to the door, and steps at the door. | Aerial photo, Scan position | slow |
| [Daycare capacity](/docs/tools/daycare-capacity/) | How many children this space could be licensed for: indoor room area, outdoor play area, toilets and sinks against the Texas or California rule, with the limiting factor named. | Rooms, Room names, Aerial property facts | slow |
| [Daylight](/docs/tools/daylight/) | Windows, glass area, the way each room faces and its hours of direct sun on a chosen day. | Doors and windows | fast |
| [Derived fields](/docs/tools/derived-fields/) | Proposes the record fields this property lacks, measured from the scan and the aerial photo. | Rooms | slow |
| [Door widths](/docs/tools/door-widths/) | Measures every doorway and flags the ones too narrow for you or what you are moving. | Doors and windows | fast |
| [Driveway fit](/docs/tools/driveway-fit/) | Estimates the driveway's length and width from the aerial photo, how many cars park on it, and whether a trailer, RV or boat fits off the street. | Aerial photo, Aerial property facts | slow |
| [Egress routes](/docs/tools/egress-routes/) | The way out of every room: how far, how narrow, which rooms have one way out, and the hose stretch. | Doors and windows | fast |
| [Facility audit](/docs/tools/facility-audit/) | Your building in one pass: doors, restrooms, ways out, life-safety equipment seen, occupant load and cleaning cost, ending in a prioritised fix list with rough costs. | Doors and windows | slow |
| [Ask the facts database](/docs/tools/facts-query/) | Answers questions across every scanned building that no fixed tool covers, with the SQL shown. | Facts database | fast |
| [Feng shui reading](/docs/tools/feng-shui/) | How a feng shui practitioner would read this home: the front door's direction, the Bagua grid on each floor, the command position for beds and desks, and the usual layout notes. A cultural tradition, not a measured effect. | Wall outline, Rooms | fast |
| [Field audit](/docs/tools/field-audit/) | Across many scans, which record fields disagree with what the scans measure, ranked, with evidence. | Property record, Rooms | slow |
| [Field check](/docs/tools/field-check/) | Lists what the scan could not settle and a person on site can, and records what they found: confirmed, contradicted or measured. | phone.capture | fast |
| [Fire pre-plan](/docs/tools/fire-preplan/) | Writes the pre-incident plan from the scans, the aerial photo and the property record, with a one-page printable sheet. | Rooms | slow |
| [Flood rehearsal](/docs/tools/flood-rehearsal/) | Shows what a flood does inside this house: how deep in each room, which doors and windows go under, and the order rooms flood, with the floor-height error carried through. | Rooms | slow |
| [Floor height](/docs/tools/floor-height/) | Narrows the floor's height above the ground, the flood result's widest uncertainty, from steps counted and fixes taken at your own front door. | Rooms | slow |
| [Furniture fit](/docs/tools/furniture-fit/) | Checks whether a sofa, bed, piano or fridge can be carried from the entrance to a room: each door and turn with its margin, and where it would stick. | Wall outline, Doors and windows | fast |
| [Garage fit](/docs/tools/garage-fit/) | Checks whether your car, truck, van or trailer fits in a scanned garage: how many side by side, the room to open doors, headroom for a lift or roof box, and room for a bench or charger. | Wall outline, Rooms | fast |
| [Hazard profile](/docs/tools/hazard-profile/) | Every hazard map at an address in one place: FEMA flood zone, hurricane evacuation zone, storm surge, Hurricane Harvey's high-water marks nearby, sea level rise, earthquake faults, liquefaction and landslide zones, fire hazard zones, urban heat, traffic noise, and weather alerts, earthquakes and wildfires now. | public.hazards | fast |

| [Smoke alarm census](/docs/tools/life-safety-census/) | Checks every bedroom's ceiling photo for a smoke alarm and lists where none was seen, as leads to check. | 360° photos, Room names | slow |
| [Listing claims](/docs/tools/listing-claims/) | Checks what a listing says against the scan, and lists what it leaves out. | Rooms | fast |
| [Loading docks](/docs/tools/loading-docks/) | Counts dock doors and drive-in doors on each wall from the oblique aerial views and beside the CoStar record. | Angled aerial photos | slow |
| [Market comps](/docs/tools/market-comps/) | Comparable properties from CoStar: asking rent, sale price per ft², class, year built and space for lease, and where this building sits against them. | Property record | fast |
| [Measure search](/docs/tools/measure-search/) | Finds homes by what is measured inside them: door widths on the way to the bathroom, turning space, wall lengths, floors, steps. No listing site can search this. | Measures table | fast |

| [Pet fit](/docs/tools/pet-fit/) | Whether a home suits your dog, cat or small animal: stairs, floors, a crate and litter spot, a pet door, fall-risk windows, the yard's size, slope, shade and fence, the pool, busy roads, the pet policy and fees, and dog parks and vets nearby. | Rooms | slow |
